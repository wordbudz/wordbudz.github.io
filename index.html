WordBudz — Free Daily Word Association Game

WordBudz is a free voice-based word association game that tests how fast your brain makes connections. You are given five words and have five seconds to say an associated word for each one. Your responses are scored in real time using natural language processing and vector similarity.

How to Play

A word appears on screen. Say the first associated word that comes to mind within five seconds. Your response is scored out of 1.0 based on how closely it associates with the prompt word. Maximum total score per round is 5.0.

Daily Word Puzzle

A new set of words is available every day. Come back daily to keep your streak, improve your average score, and climb the leaderboard.
